CodexBar 2.1.0

Antigravity is available as an experimental provider. After a notice, you sign in to Google in an in-app browser and CodexBar shows the weekly and five-hour limits that Google's own Models & Usage page reports for Gemini Models and for Claude and GPT Models. Google doesn't officially support third-party tools reading that page, and its terms allow account suspension.

Google Gemini chat limits and the Antigravity sign-in from version 2.0 are no longer supported. Google accounts, credentials and website sessions saved by version 2.0 are removed on upgrade; add Antigravity again to use the new provider. Your other accounts, groups and usage history remain available.

Greptile is no longer supported. Updating removes a saved Greptile account and its usage history from this device, and CodexBar shows a one-time note when it does. Your other accounts are unchanged.

Claude cards show the plan reported by your organization, including Ultra and Max where supported. Saved resets say what each one restores, including Fable, and each usable reset has its own Use action. More Information shows promotional credit when Claude reports it.

The dashboard and weekly quota details focus on usage and reset times, with subscription scores removed. Missing Grok and X Subscription usage stays unavailable instead of appearing as zero.

Claude billing opens the provider's own page. Automatic use of saved Claude billing sessions is paused; saved data remains until you disconnect. This does not supply an automatic renewal date.

CodexBar now sends its own name as the user agent of its Claude requests, and Claude sign-in asks for fewer permissions. Accounts already signed in are not signed out or changed. If Claude declines a request, the card says so and does not sign you out. Claude may limit usage or saved resets for apps other than its own; the card says so when it does.

Provider data depends on your account and service. This update does not add Muse Code usage on iPhone.
