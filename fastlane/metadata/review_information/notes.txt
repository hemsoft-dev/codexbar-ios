CodexBar is a local dashboard for read-only access to user-owned provider accounts. It has no CodexBar account or HemSoft backend. Apple processes Pro purchases with StoreKit.

The free plan allows one active account and seven days of history. Pro adds all saved accounts, alerts, widgets, Apple Watch and complications, and 90-day history. Open Settings > CodexBar Pro. Review the StoreKit price, then tap Choose monthly, Choose yearly, Start 7-day free trial when eligible, or Buy lifetime. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are on that screen. No provider account is needed to reach it.

Yearly has a seven-day free introductory trial for eligible accounts; monthly and lifetime do not. StoreKit supplies localized prices. Lifetime is non-consumable. Family Sharing is off for all three. Subscriptions use Apple's management sheet. Switching from a subscription to lifetime requires auto-renew off; lifetime does not cancel or refund a subscription. Manage Payment Method opens Apple's billing page.

Original-purchaser lifetime Pro requires verified, non-revoked production AppTransaction evidence under the documented build/version/date policy. Sandbox and TestFlight AppTransactions do not qualify. The compiled family cutoff is November 5, 2026 at 7:00 PM EST, 2026-11-06T00:00:00Z. Restore calls AppStore.sync after a tap, with AppTransaction.refresh for recovery when needed. Unverified purchase information blocks another charge; use Restore Purchases or Refresh Purchase Information.

The app opens without an account. Live cards require reviewer-owned provider accounts or API keys. No shared credentials are supplied. Add a connection from Settings > Accounts & Groups > Add Account. Connections can be removed independently. Synthetic values are development-only; there is no customer demo mode.

ChatGPT / Codex and OpenCode Go + Zen offer browser or private sign-in; OpenCode approval includes workspace selection. Gemini uses a private Google website for consumer usage and OAuth with PKCE for coding usage, linked after same-account confirmation. Grok and X Subscription use xAI device authorization and verify identity and consumer usage before saving a token. Service permissions or subscription limits can prevent live metrics.

Greptile review activity and billing renewal are separate. API-key accounts retain review activity. Billing renewal needs an account-scoped dashboard session saved in Keychain. Fresh Google-backed billing sign-in or reconnection may be unavailable. Native OAuth does not authorize the billing endpoint. Saved verified sessions can return renewal dates. No manual cookie or token transfer is offered.

GitHub Billing sign-in is separate from Copilot. The disclosed repo, admin:org and user scopes permit broader changes, but CodexBar only reads billing data. It does not modify repositories, organizations, profiles or follows.

Provider credentials can grant broader access than monitoring. The app discloses this, stores them separately per account in Keychain, and uses them only for documented authorization, renewal and read-only requests. Disconnect removes the local credential, not the provider grant.

Pro widgets and Watch show saved provider snapshots plus access-validity state, never credentials. The Watch does not request provider data. Apple controls background refresh. GitHub service-status checks use its public endpoint without credentials. Optional feedback previews non-secret categories before opening an email draft or public GitHub form; the user sends or submits it. No advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK is included.
